<EssayLead eyebrow="AI · POWER · POLITICAL ORDER" title={['当人类不再是', '社会运转所必需的']}>

今天，这个世界终究是由人构成的。

</EssayLead>

这给了普通人一点点制衡的力量：工人们能够破坏机器让生产停摆；卡车司机可以罢工，让整个经济陷入停滞；警察可以袖手旁观，任由[一场抗议活动推翻政府](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromaidan)；军队也可以[哗变](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiel_mutiny)。这些限制了强者欺压弱者的程度：无论他们多么有权势，无论拥有多少财富，无论麾下有多少师，他们终究仍然嵌入在社会中。他们需要其他人才能存活，维持他们生活的质量。而这些人只要撤回自己对这个秩序的参与，实质上就拥有否决整个政治秩序的能力。

AI 改变了这一切。倘若工厂由 AI 运转，卡车由 AI 驾驶，大部分白领的工作由 AI 承担，战争也由 AI 去打，那么人类在物质层面上就会失去力量。我们将失去一种用来制衡权力的杠杆。我们会[被剥夺力量](https://intelligence-curse.ai/pyramid/)。当人们谈论 AI 夺走工作时，他们主要担忧的问题是贫穷和意义，但是我认为应该有更多人真正担心的是这种失权。

但如果根本不会出现大规模失业呢？毕竟，过去两百年来，我们一直在把一个又一个工作自动化，而如今每一个发达经济体事实上都接近充分就业。我们不断找到新的、更高层次、薪酬也更高的工作可做。因此，也许 AI 只是接手了今天的工作，而人类则再往上走一层，去从事更新的工作。[Alex Imas](https://x.com/alexolegimas) 在[《什么会变得稀缺？》](https://aleximas.substack.com/p/what-will-be-scarce)一文中这样描述这一前景：

> 随着标准化商品生产逐步实现自动化，收入与就业会流向那些收入弹性较高的部门——我把它们称为“关系型部门”。其中包括艺术，也包括照护、教育、酒店与接待、心理治疗、个性化服务、手工技艺以及社区活动；在这些领域，人的存在本身就是价值的一部分。
>
> ……
>
> 如果这个模型是对的，那么未来真正经久不衰的工作，不会是监控 AI 系统，也不会是提示词工程。这些只不过是自动化部门中的过渡性岗位。真正持久的工作会存在于关系型部门，在那里，人的存在本身就是产品。
>
> 其中一些工作早已存在，而且还在增长：护士、治疗师、教师、精品健身教练、私人厨师、定制裁缝、精酿啤酒师、现场表演者、精神导师、托育工作者，以及形形色色的酒店接待与照护工作。另一些则正在出现：体验设计师、人机协作艺术家、来源真实性认证人员、社区策展人。还有许多工作甚至尚未被发明出来——就像今天人们从事的工作中，有六成在 1940 年时还不存在一样。

所以，在这样的世界里，我们会经历大规模的岗位替代，却不会经历大规模失业。而我的观点是：即便生活在这样的世界里，人类依然会失去制衡权力的能力。

<Original label="开篇 · 英文原文">

Today, the world is made of people. This gives people a tiny measure of leverage: factory workers can sabotage the machines and halt production, truck drivers can strike and halt the entire economy, the police can stand back and [let a protest topple the government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euromaidan), the military can [mutiny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiel_mutiny). This sets a limit to how much the powerful can abuse the powerless: no matter how powerful someone is, how much money they have, how many divisions they command, they are still embedded in a society. They need other people to survive, and to maintain their quality of life. And those people can, essentially, veto the political order by withdrawing their contribution to it.

AI changes this. If AI works the factories, drives the trucks, does most of the white collar work, fights the wars, then humans become materially powerless. We lose one type of leverage against power. We become [disempowered](https://intelligence-curse.ai/pyramid/). When people talk about AI taking jobs, the main problems they worry about are poverty and meaning, but I think more people should be worried about disempowerment.

But what if there is no mass unemployment? After all, we’ve spent two hundred years automating one job after another, and every advanced economy is _de facto_ at full employment. We keep discovering new, higher-level, better-paying jobs to do. So maybe the AIs take today’s jobs, and humans move one level up, to work newer jobs. [Alex Imas](https://x.com/alexolegimas), in [_What will be scarce?_](https://aleximas.substack.com/p/what-will-be-scarce), describes what this looks like:

> As commodity production gets automated, income and employment flow toward the sectors with high income elasticity: what I am calling the relational sector, including the arts but also care, education, hospitality, therapy, personal services, craftsmanship, and community, where the human element is part of the value.
>
> […]
>
> If the model is right, the durable jobs of the future won’t be about monitoring AI systems or prompt engineering. Those are transitional roles in the automated sector. The durable jobs will be in the relational sector, where the human element is the product itself.
>
> Some already exist and are growing: nurses, therapists, teachers, boutique fitness instructors, personal chefs, bespoke tailors, craft brewers, live performers, spiritual guides, childcare workers, and many varieties of hospitality and care work. Others are emerging: experience designers, human-AI collaboration artists, provenance certifiers, community curators. Many haven’t been invented yet, just as six out of ten jobs people hold today didn’t exist in 1940.

</Original>

<Section index="01" kicker="The argument">

## 失去工作并不是最严重的问题

</Section>

如果全世界的播客主播都罢工了，什么会发生呢？什么都不会发生。精品健身教练，私人厨师，精酿啤酒师之类的职业也是如此。“关系型经济”的问题在于：这些工作通常比制造业轻松得多，也许薪酬会更多，但它们让人离物质现实更远。流水线上的工人可以破坏机器。然而一个坐在全国另一头办公室的企业高管，他们又拥有多少物质上的力量？总不至于说，所有工厂都预先装好了爆破装置，好让老板们在企业面临国有化时一拉某根“紧急拉杆”就把工厂炸掉吧。我怀疑就连马斯克也不会这么干。

<Concept term="Material leverage" zh="物质杠杆">

一个人或群体因为掌握生产、运输、能源、暴力或其他关键物质节点，而拥有让政治秩序付出实际代价的能力。不是仅仅“拥有意见”，而是能够让现实世界因为你的拒绝合作而发生变化。

</Concept>

从某种意义上说，这恰好是中世纪农民处境的镜像。农民拥有巨大的物质杠杆——毕竟所有粮食都是他们种出来的——但他们不识字、贫穷，而且散居各处，因此无法彼此协调，也就无法真正运用这种杠杆。工业社会中的人则识字、相对富裕，并且集中生活在城市中心：他们既拥有物质杠杆，也有能力把这种杠杆转化为实际力量。到了后 AI 社会，人们将始终保持彼此联络，也可能人人识字、受过教育，但他们将不再拥有任何物质力量。于是，他们也将再无办法通过现实世界中的行动，撤回被统治者对统治秩序的同意。

在后 AGI 世界里，人们将再无办法撤回被统治者的同意。他们无法通过经济手段抵抗，因为从经济体系的角度看，他们已经不再是必需的：即使他们仍然有工作，那些工作也不会是物质系统不可或缺的岗位。即便他们仍有可能抵抗，也无法协调这种抵抗，因为 AGI 会让大规模监控——其所需的物理与法律基础设施其实早已存在——变得有效上千倍。因为一旦智能的成本低廉到几乎可以忽略，就完全可以给每一个人都配上一名能力远超人类的情报分析员，专门盯着他。而如果战争由 AI 来打，那么内战同样可以由 AI 来打。因此，人们也无法通过暴力进行抵抗，因为由强大 AGI 控制的无人机，几乎肯定会比手持步枪的人类拥有高得多的击杀交换比，而且造价还更低。

<Aside>

真正有力量的“同意”，必须包含撤回同意的能力。

</Aside>

那么，在这样的世界里，究竟谁拥有权力？是那极少数能够向强大 AI 下达命令的人——而这些 AI 掌控着杀戮机器。但这种局面恐怕也维持不了多久，因为迟早有一天，这些人也会被他们自己的 AI 取而代之。

当然，自由民主制度也许仍有可能继续保持自由与民主，即使它已不再真正需要选民。但那听起来更像是一种极其脆弱的安排：在这种安排下，自由民主变成了一个[“副产物式结构”](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spandrel_(biology)>)，它之所以继续存在，与其说是因为仍有现实上的必要，不如说只是因为制度惯性还在推动它延续。

<Original label="观点 · 英文原文">

If all the podcasters in the world go on strike, what happens? Nothing. Same for the boutique fitness instructors, personal chefs, craft brewers, etc. The problem with the “relational economy” is that while these jobs are generally nicer than working in manufacturing, and might pay better, they put you further away from material reality. A worker at an assembly line can sabotage the machines; whereas an executive sitting in an office on the other side of the country, what _material_ power do they have? It’s not like the factories are all rigged to blow, as a “pull lever in case of nationalization” contingency. I doubt even Elon would do that.

In a sense it’s the dual version of being a medieval peasant. Peasants had a lot of material leverage—they grew all the food—but were illiterate and poor and dispersed, so they could not coordinate to exploit that leverage. People in industrial societies are literate, relatively wealthy, and concentrated in urban centers: they have material leverage and the ability to exploit it. People in post-AI societies will be in constant communication, they may be literate and educated, but they will have no material power whatever. And so they will have no way to physically withdraw the consent of the governed.

In the post-AGI world, people have no way to withdraw the consent of the governed. They can’t resist economically, because they are economically unnecessary: if they still have jobs, they won’t be materially vital jobs. Even if they could resist, they can’t coordinate their resistance, because AGI will make mass surveillance (the physical and legal infrastructure for which already exists) a thousandfold more effective, since with intelligence too cheap to meter, you can have a superhumanly-capable intelligence analyst assigned to each human. And if the AIs fight the wars, they can also fight the civil wars. So people can’t resist violently either, because drones controlled by powerful AGI almost certainly have a better K/D ratio than humans with rifles (and are cheaper to build).

Who has power in this world? The few humans who give orders to the powerful AIs that control the killing machines. And probably not for long, since sooner or later those humans will be usurped by their AIs.

It might be the case that liberal democracies can remain liberal and democratic despite not needing their voters. But that sounds like a fragile arrangement, in which liberal democracy is a [spandrel](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spandrel_(biology)>), which exists more because of inertia than because it’s necessary.

</Original>

<Section index="02" kicker="Objections">

## 如果这一切根本不会发生呢？

</Section>

如果我们真的造出了强大的 AGI，但前面所说的这一切都没有发生，那么问题就是：为什么没有发生？下面是几种可能性，按我认为的可能性由高到低排列：

<div className="essay-scenarios">

<Scenario index="01" title="什么都没有真正改变" en="Nothing ever happens">

也许由于制度本身的惯性或韧性，我们会进入这样一个拥有强大 AGI 的世界：其中每一个参与者都有动力让现有体系继续运转。比如，婴儿潮一代即使已经不再工作，也仍可能是每个西方国家最有权势的群体，因为那些掌握工作岗位和武力的人并不希望社会陷入混乱。也许这种状态能够维持足够长的时间，让我们跌跌撞撞地熬过那段最危险的时期。

</Scenario>

<Scenario index="02" title="持久的互补关系" en="Enduring complementarity">

也许 AGI 里的那个“G”根本就[不是真实存在的东西](https://x.com/xuanalogue/status/2072101307695919278)；每一种心智其实都有自己不同的[“形状”](https://helentoner.substack.com/p/taking-jaggedness-seriously)。今天，AI 很擅长写代码，却很不擅长写文章。也许这种参差不齐的能力结构会无限期延续下去：于是，那些能够通过基准测试一路优化到极致的工作会被 AI 接管，而人类则继续承担那些更抽象、需要极高样本效率的工作。

</Scenario>

<Scenario index="03" title="经典毁灭结局" en="Classic doom">

我们造出了强大的 ASI。

然后所有人都死了。

</Scenario>

<Scenario index="04" title="系统管理员情景" en="The sysop scenario">

我们造出了强大的 ASI，但它是对齐的，于是它[接管世界，并建立起一个无法逃脱、永久存在的功利主义反乌托邦](https://web.archive.org/web/20001017124429/http://www.singinst.org/intro.html)。我们没有死，但也同样失去了对未来的控制，最终变成了某种类似宠物的存在。另可参见[《Prime Intellect 的蜕变》](https://localroger.com/prime-intellect/)。

</Scenario>

<Scenario index="05" title="深度学习撞上天花板" en="Deep learning hits a wall">

结果大致与上面类似，只不过原因不同。

</Scenario>

</div>

<Original label="反对者们 · 英文原文">

If we get powerful AGI, but none of this happens: why _didn’t_ it happen? Some possibilities, sorted by decreasing likelihood:

- **Nothing ever happens:** because of institutional inertia/robustness, maybe we get a world with powerful AGI where every actor is incentivized to keep the system going, e.g. Boomers don’t have jobs but they’re the most powerful group in every Western country because the people with the jobs and the guns don’t want chaos. And maybe this lasts long enough that we can muddle through the dangerous period.
- **Enduring complementarity:** the “G” in AGI [isn’t a real thing](https://x.com/xuanalogue/status/2072101307695919278): every mind just has a different [shape](https://helentoner.substack.com/p/taking-jaggedness-seriously). Today, AI is great at coding and terrible at prose. Maybe this continues indefinitely into the future, and so the AIs take the jobs that can be benchmaxxed, and humans do the more abstract jobs that require high sample efficiency.
- **Classic doom:** we get powerful ASI, and we all die.
- **The sysop scenario:** we get powerful ASI, but it’s aligned, so it [takes over the world, and installs an inescapable permanent utilitarian dystopia](https://web.archive.org/web/20001017124429/http://www.singinst.org/intro.html). We don’t die, but we don’t have control over the future either, and we become something like pets. See also: [_The Metamorphosis of Prime Intellect_](https://localroger.com/prime-intellect/).
- **Deep learning hits a wall:** more or less as above, but for different reasons.

</Original>

<Section index="03" kicker="A more precise argument">

## 到底什么才叫拥有力量？

</Section>

如果你已经被前面的论证说服了，那读到这里其实就可以停下了。但问题是，写完这篇文章的第一版之后，连我自己都还没有被说服。人类读者和 Claude 们提出了不少反驳，其中有一些我觉得相当重要。难道我是在自欺欺人吗？于是我坐下来，又认真想了一遍：所谓“人们拥有力量”，究竟是什么意思？所谓“失权”又是什么意思？它真的能够被严谨地定义吗，还是说，它其实只是一个用来触发某种情绪反应的词？

我们不妨这样定义：如果一群人能够对自己所生活国家的政治秩序产生实际影响，那么就可以说，他们拥有政治力量。民主社会中的选民至少作为一个整体拥有这种力量，而俄罗斯帝国时期的农奴则没有。

那么一般来说，究竟什么样的人拥有力量？从历史上看，在许多地方，答案往往是：那些掌握武力、拥有[群体凝聚力](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asabiyyah)，并且能够协调彼此行动的人。所以，也许我们可以把权力粗略地写成一个公式：拥有暴力能力，并且能够协调行动。

<Equation
  label='第一次近似 · 暴力与协调'
  formula={'\\text{Power} \\iff \\text{Violence} \\land \\text{Coordination}'}
  terms={[
    ['Power', '政治力量'],
    ['Violence', '暴力能力'],
    ['Coordination', '协调能力']
  ]}
/>

（那像神职人员这类既不打仗、也不从事生产，却仍然拥有权力的群体又该怎么算？好了，我们总不能一整天都耗在这里。这本来就不是一套完整的政治学理论，更何况我已经在 LaTeX 和逻辑符号上钻得够深了。）

再看现代民主社会中的选民：他们并不属于军事精英，却依然能够对政治秩序产生影响。为什么？他们拥有协调能力——这得益于大众识字和现代通信——但他们通常并不会以准军事暴力相威胁。那么，他们究竟拥有什么，可以起到暴力的替代作用？也许答案是：所有具体工作终究都由人来做，而一旦人们停止履行自己的工作，军队就会断粮，警察也拿不到工资，等等；而且与中世纪农民不同，现代人能够彼此协调行动。所以，也许我们可以把前面的公式进一步修正为：

<Equation
  label='修正版 · 劳动也可以成为杠杆'
  formula={'\\text{Power} \\iff (\\text{Violence} \\lor \\text{Work}) \\land \\text{Coordination}'}
  terms={[
    ['Violence ∨ Work', '暴力或劳动形成的物质杠杆'],
    ['∧ Coordination', '并且能够协调行动']
  ]}
/>

换句话说，一个群体若要真正拥有政治力量，需要具备两样东西：

1. 对国家形成现实制约的物质杠杆——无论这种杠杆来自暴力还是经济；
2. 协调彼此行动、从而真正运用这种杠杆的能力。

那么苏联又该怎么解释？当时的识字率很高，通信技术也相对现代化；为什么人们没有利用自己在经济上的杠杆，向国家争取政治权利？“因为国家会杀了他们”并不是一个足够好的解释，因为这个解释得出的结论太强了：照这种模型，任何国家都不可能走向自由化和民主化。我认为，一个较为合理的解释是，国家的压制足以在事实上废掉人们彼此协调行动的能力：所有通信技术都处在监控和审查之下。

那么，这套论证难道不会推出这样一个结论吗：随着一个国家的就业越来越转向服务业，它的政治也应该变得越来越威权？可现实中我们观察到的恰恰相反。不过，无论一个国家的经济中服务业占比是 5% 还是 95%，经济的物质层面都同样不可或缺。因此，随着就业越来越抽象化，社会整体所拥有的政治力量并不会因此消失。以法国为例，它的经济大约有 80% 属于服务业，但剩下那 20% 仍然可以通过罢工让整个国家陷入瘫痪。从某种意义上说，每一个知识工作者，其实都在搭那些能够让经济停摆的卡车司机和码头工人的便车。

不过，我自己也不太喜欢这个对反驳的反驳，因为它很难被证伪：按照这个说法，各国完全可能一直维持自由民主，直到 AGI 把所有从事物质性工作的人工都替代掉；到了那一刻，国家才突然来一句：[“哈，这正是我们想让你一直以为的！”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBMeMrPuQyU)然后彻底转向威权统治。

If you’re already convinced, you can stop reading. But, see, after writing the first version of this post, I wasn’t convinced. Humans and Claudes made a number of counterarguments that I felt were important. Am I fooling myself? So I sat down to think some more. What does it mean for “people” to be “empowered”? What does “disempowerment” mean? Does it mean anything rigorous, or is it just an emotional cue?

Let’s say a group of people is empowered if they have a say in the political order of the state they live in. Voters in a democratic society are empowered, at least in the aggregate, while serfs in the Russian Empire were disempowered.

Who is empowered, in general? Historically, in many places: people with weapons, [_asabiyyah_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asabiyyah), and the ability to coordinate their actions. So, maybe we can say that power equals violence plus coordination.

$$
\text{Power} \iff \text{Violence} \land \text{Coordination}
$$

(What about priests and other such groups who have power despite not fighting or working? Listen, we can’t be here all day. This isn’t meant to be a complete theory of political science and I’ve already gone too deep with the LaTeX and the logical notation.)

Now, voters in modern democracies have a say in the political order, though they are not part of a military elite. Why? They have coordination (thanks to mass literacy and modern communications) but they generally don’t threaten paramilitary violence. What do they have that can substitute for violence? Maybe it’s that people do all the work, and if they stop doing their jobs, the army runs out of food, police don’t get paid, etc., and unlike medieval peasants, modern people can coordinate. So maybe we can refine the formula to:

$$
\text{Power} \iff (\text{Violence} \lor \text{Work}) \land \text{Coordination}
$$

In other words, for a group to be empowered, they need:

1. Material leverage (of the violent or economic kind) against the state
2. The ability to coordinate to exploit that leverage.

What about the Soviet Union? They had high rates of literacy, and relatively modern communication technology: why didn’t the people exploit their economic leverage against the state to gain political rights? “Because the state would kill them” is not a good enough explanation because it proves too much: under that model, no country would liberalize and democratize. I think a reasonable explanation is that the state’s repression was enough to effectively neuter people’s ability to coordinate: every communication technology was surveilled and censored.

Now, doesn’t this argument imply that, as a country’s employment moves towards services, the country’s politics should become more authoritarian, while in reality we observe the opposite? But whether a country’s economy is 5% or 95% services, the material side of the economy is equally vital. So “aggregate empowerment” remains as employment becomes more and more abstract. Consider France, where the economy is ~80% services, but the last 20% can still paralyze the country through strikes. In a sense every knowledge worker is free-riding on the ability of truck drivers and dock workers to shut down the economy. I don’t greatly like this counter-counterargument, though, because it’s hard to falsify: it implies that countries might remain liberal democracies _until_ AGI replaces all humans in material work, at which point the state can go [“that’s what we wanted you to think!”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBMeMrPuQyU) and go full authoritarian.
